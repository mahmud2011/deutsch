# Fragen mit Fragewort

## Rules

| Position 1 (Fragewort) | Position 2 (Verb) | Position 3 (Subjekt) |
|------------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Wie                    | heißen            | Sie?                 |
| Wo                     | ist               | er?                  |

The question word is always in **position 1** and always starts with **w**.


## Fragewörter

| Fragewort       | English      | Example                                                          |
|-----------------|--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Wer             | Who          | Wer ist das? (Who is that?)                                      |
| Was             | What         | Was hat er gemacht? (What did he do?)                            |
| Wo              | Where        | Wo ist er? (Where is he?)                                        |
| Woher           | Where from   | Woher kommt er? (Where is he from?)                              |
| Wohin           | Where to     | Wohin will er gehen? (Where does he want to go?)                 |
| Wann            | When         | Wann kommt er? (When is he coming?)                              |
| Wie             | How          | Wie heißen Sie? (What is your name?)                             |
| Wie viele       | How many     | Wie viele Taschen hat er? (How many bags does he have?)          |
| Um wie viel Uhr | At what time | Um wie viel Uhr geht er? (At what time does he leave?)           |
| Warum           | Why          | Warum trägt er einen Hut? (Why does he wear a hat?)              |
| Wen             | Whom (acc.)  | Wen sucht er? (Whom is he looking for?)                          |
| Mit wem         | With whom    | Mit wem spricht er? (Who is he talking to?)                      |


## Examples

**Price** — both are correct:
- Was kostet das? — Ein Kilo kostet 3,50 Euro. (What does it cost?)
- Wie viel kosten die Tabletten? — 3,50 Euro. (How much do the tablets cost?)

**Time** — both are correct:
- Wann beginnt der Film? — Um acht. (When does the film start?)
- Um wie viel Uhr beginnt der Film? — Um acht Uhr. (At what time does the film start?) — `Um` belongs here, not with `Wann`

**Profession** — all correct:
- Was sind Sie von Beruf? (What is your profession? — formal)
- Was bist du von Beruf? (What is your profession? — informal)
- Was machen Sie beruflich? (What do you do professionally?)
